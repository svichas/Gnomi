# Workspace administrator tickets, spring round

Invented for the Gym demo. No real person said any of this.

## Notes

Administrators set the tool up once and come back when something changes: a new hire, a new approval rule, an audit. They read settings pages closely and worry more about the wrong person getting access than about speed. They say "permissions" and "approval chain".

Lines from support tickets:

I need to see who can approve a job before I invite anyone else.

I invited a manager last week and I cannot tell whether the invite ever arrived.

Changing a setting should say who it affects, because I answer for it in the audit.
