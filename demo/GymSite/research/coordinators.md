# Department coordinator interviews, spring round

Invented for the Gym demo. No real person said any of this.

## Notes

Coordinators do not own any job. They keep several hiring managers moving, check what is waiting for a review each morning and chase people by chat. They want one list across every open job, and they copy names into their own notes.

Quotes from the sessions:

I start every morning by asking which candidates have been waiting the longest.

I do not own any of these jobs, I just keep them moving.

When the list hides closed jobs I can never tell if a job is gone or just filtered out.
