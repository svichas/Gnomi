## Notes

Recruiters post several jobs a week and reuse the wording of older posts. Most keep a text file of descriptions open beside the form. They talk about "reqs" and "the hiring manager" rather than "positions" or "owners".

Quotes from the sessions:

I write the description somewhere else first, because I have lost a whole draft to a form before.

If the hiring manager has no account yet, I need to know before I press publish, not after.

Every req has a budget code, and if the form hides that field I go looking for it.

We still track who is waiting on what in a spreadsheet, whatever the tool says.
