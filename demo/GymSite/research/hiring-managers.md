# Hiring manager interviews, spring round

Invented for the Gym demo. No real person said any of this.

## Notes

Hiring managers open the tool a few times a month, usually from an email and often on a phone between meetings. They read the resume first and want the decision to take a minute. They say "shortlist" and "pass", and few know what a pipeline stage is called in the product.

Quotes from the sessions:

I open it from the email on my phone, read the resume, and I want to be done before my next meeting.

Tell me whose turn it is, because I only come here when someone asks me to.

I wrote two paragraphs of feedback once and I still do not know if anyone saw them.

If I cannot tell whether my decision saved, I send the recruiter a message instead.
