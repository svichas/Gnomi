WHAT CHANGED

trait sampling, four model-written fictional candidates, selection and biography
expansion, then human review. Profiles encode assumptions about an audience.
The report chapter distinguishes simulation records from evidence about people.
Generated comments and quotes are identified as synthetic. Counts and social
spread do not establish human needs, prevalence or word-of-mouth reach.
The closing validation chapter explicitly says: hypotheses, not evidence.
Its question mark replaces the earlier checkmark. The film's ceramic figures,
paper/ink/olive palette, existing product captures and ten-chapter structure remain.

REBUILD (from repository root)
1. npm ci --prefix videos/output/gnomi-film/source
   Install Playwright Chromium if it is not already available.
2. python3 videos/output/gnomi-film/source/prepare-audio.py
3. node videos/output/gnomi-film/source/render.mjs stills
4. node videos/output/gnomi-film/source/render.mjs video
5. ffmpeg -y -f concat -safe 0 -i videos/output/gnomi-film/source/concat-v4.txt -c copy videos/output/gnomi-film/visuals-v4.mp4
6. Open Gnomi.aive in Synthcut; export Gnomi-master.mp4 at 1080p/24fps,
   H.264 CRF 18, AAC, target -16 LUFS and -1 dB true peak.
7. python3 videos/output/gnomi-film/source/package.py
