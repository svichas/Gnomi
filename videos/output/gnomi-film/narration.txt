You know your product. But what happens when someone sees it for the first time?

Meet Gnomi. Social simulation for product teams. Perspective before you ship.

Start with your audience. Define the roles, languages, devices, and goals that matter. Gnomi turns those differences into individual simulated people.

Share your idea, staging app, or your Figma design. Set a goal and a budget. Each persona explores from a different perspective.

They click. They hesitate. They hit friction. And you can follow the actions, thoughts, and evidence behind every step.

Then the conversation starts. Personas post, reply, and react. One confusing experience can become a shared opinion. Watch where it travels, and whose view changes.

Gnomi brings it together in a report: ranked potential frictions, affected segments, screenshots, and quotes. See what happened, why it matters, and what you could change.

Projects run on your machine, with your own model keys. Model inputs go to the provider you configure.

These are simulated findings. Use them as hypotheses, then validate important decisions with real people.

Find the friction. Follow the conversation. Know what to test next. Gnomi.
