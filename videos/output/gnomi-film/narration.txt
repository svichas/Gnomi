You know your product. But what happens when someone sees it for the first time?

Meet Gnomi. Social simulation for product teams. Perspective before you ship.

You define audience segments and their shares. Gnomi samples traits such as language, device, and patience using a saved random seed. A language model proposes fictional profiles from those traits. Gnomi selects one candidate and expands its biography. You review and edit the personas before a run. They represent your assumptions about an audience.

Share your idea, staging app, or your Figma design. Set a goal and a budget. Each persona explores from a different perspective.

They click. They pause. They encounter friction. Follow their actions, generated comments, and screenshots to inspect what happened in the simulation.

Then the conversation starts. Personas post, reply, and react. Watch how a complaint travels through this simulated community, and whose generated opinion changes.

Gnomi brings it together in a report: ranked friction hypotheses, affected simulated segments, screenshots, and generated quotes. These records show what the agents did. They do not establish what real users need or how often a problem occurs.

Projects run on your machine, with your own model keys. Model inputs go to the provider you configure.

Simulated findings are hypotheses, not evidence about real users. Choose what to investigate, then test it with real people before drawing conclusions.

Find the friction. Follow the conversation. Know what to test next. Gnomi.
